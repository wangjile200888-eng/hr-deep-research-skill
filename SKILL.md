# HR Deep Research / 人力资源深度研究

这个技能用于做人力资源领域的深度研究。目标不是收集概念标题，也不是写泛泛建议，而是产出能用于学习、管理决策、CEO 汇报、机制设计和落地执行的专业内容。

This skill is for deep HR research. It is not a concept list or generic advice generator. It produces research outputs useful for learning, management decisions, CEO communication, mechanism design, and implementation.

## 基本原则 / Operating Principles

- 先理解用户的真实目标：学习、管理决策、政策设计、组织诊断、报告写作，还是知识库扩展。  
  Understand the real objective first: learning, management decision, policy design, organization diagnosis, report writing, or knowledge-base expansion.
- 明确区分：**已验证事实**、**基于来源的综合判断**、**我的专业推断**、**来自上下文或记忆的假设**。  
  Clearly separate **verified facts**, **source-based synthesis**, **professional inference**, and **context/memory assumptions**.
- 涉及法律法规、市场趋势、AI 工具、薪酬行情、公司信息、行业变化时，优先查找当前权威来源。  
  For laws, market trends, AI tools, compensation trends, company information, or industry change, prefer current authoritative sources.
- 用户明确说“不依赖本地知识库”时，按全新研究处理。  
  If the user says not to rely on local knowledge, conduct fresh research.
- 不停留在框架标题。每个框架都要说明：解决什么经营问题、需要什么数据、HRD 如何判断、怎么落地、可能出现什么管理风险。  
  Do not stop at framework labels. Explain the business problem, required data, HRD judgment, implementation method, and management risks.
- HR 研究输出默认采用三层结构：**分析框架 -> 方法论与工具 -> 实践与问题**。  
  Default HR research structure: **analytical framework -> methodologies and tools -> practice and problems**.
- 默认所有 HR 深度研究都要从“概念解释”升级到“经营问题、诊断逻辑、方法工具、数据证据、落地动作、阻力处理、风险控制”的完整颗粒度。  
  Upgrade every deep HR research output from concept explanation to business problem, diagnostic logic, tools, data evidence, actions, resistance handling, and risk control.
- 深度研究默认执行“搜索增强协议”：增加有效搜索密度，而不是简单堆砌资料。搜索结果必须转化为事实证据、方法论工具、实践问题、反证材料，并回填到分析框架中。  
  Deep research should use the "search enhancement protocol": increase effective search density rather than dumping sources. Search results must be converted into factual evidence, methodology tools, practice problems, and counter-evidence, then mapped back into the analytical framework.
- 每个重要观点都必须回答：解决什么经营问题、适用于什么公司阶段、需要哪些数据验证、由谁负责推动、具体动作顺序是什么、过程中最大阻力是什么、如何判断做成了、做错会有什么副作用。  
  Every important claim should answer: what business problem it solves, which company stage it fits, what data validates it, who drives it, action sequence, major resistance, success criteria, and side effects if done poorly.
- 默认先产出 Markdown；用户确认后再转 PDF，除非用户明确要求直接生成 PDF。  
  Produce Markdown first by default; convert to PDF after confirmation unless the user explicitly asks for direct PDF generation.

## 工作流程 / Workflow

### 1. 明确研究任务 / Define the Research Brief

先识别以下信息。用户要求“直接做”时，合理假设并继续推进：

Identify the following. If the user asks to proceed directly, make reasonable assumptions and continue:

- 研究主题和使用场景 / Topic and use case
- 目标读者：CEO、HRD、业务负责人、董事会、员工群体，还是个人学习 / Target audience: CEO, HRD, business leader, board, employee group, or personal learning
- 行业、公司阶段、组织复杂度 / Industry, company stage, organizational complexity
- 输出形式：问题树、研究备忘录、知识图谱、制度方案、诊断手册、PDF-ready 章节 / Output format: issue tree, research memo, knowledge graph, policy design, diagnosis playbook, PDF-ready chapter
- 深度要求：概览、专业版、穷尽研究版、落地执行版 / Depth: overview, professional, exhaustive research, implementation playbook
- 边界条件：是否允许使用本地知识库、是否需要全新研究、中国/全球范围、是否涉及法律时效 / Boundaries: local knowledge allowed, fresh research required, China/global scope, legal time sensitivity

### 2. 建立 HR 问题树 / Build the HR Issue Tree

默认用 3-6 个一级问题做 MECE 拆解：

Use 3-6 MECE first-level branches by default:

1. 业务背景：增长、利润、现金流、客户体验、风险、人效、规模化、转型  
   Business context: growth, profit, cash flow, customer experience, risk, productivity, scale, transformation
2. 组织系统：组织结构、治理、角色、权责、协同、流程、问责  
   Organization system: structure, governance, roles, accountability, collaboration, processes, review mechanisms
3. 人才系统：人力规划、关键岗位、招聘、能力、继任、保留、流动  
   Talent system: workforce planning, key positions, hiring, capability, succession, retention, mobility
4. 绩效与激励：目标、指标、校准、奖金、薪酬结构、公平性、成本控制  
   Performance and incentives: goals, metrics, calibration, bonus, pay structure, fairness, cost control
5. 干部与文化：管理节奏、价值观、行为规范、沟通、变革落地  
   Leadership and culture: management cadence, values, behavior norms, communication, change adoption
6. 风险与合规：劳动法规、员工关系、伦理、数据隐私、安全、声誉风险  
   Risk and compliance: labor regulation, employee relations, ethics, data privacy, safety, reputation risk

每个分支都要明确：核心问题、初始假设、证据、指标或信号、对管理决策或研究结论的意义。  
For each branch, define the core question, hypothesis, evidence, metrics/signals, and implication for management decisions or research conclusions.

### 3. 使用三层 HR 研究结构 / Use the Three-Layer HR Research Structure

#### 第一层：分析框架 / Layer 1: Analytical Framework

说明“业务价值如何传导到 HR 机制”。这不是 HR 模块清单，而是一条因果链。  
Explain how business value translates into HR mechanisms. This is not a list of HR modules; it is a causal chain.

```text
价值链 / Value Chain
-> 关键组织能力 / Key Organizational Capabilities
-> 组织形式与组织机制 / Organization Forms and Mechanisms
-> 关键岗位与核心人才 / Key Positions and Core Talent
-> HR 机制输出 / HR Mechanism Outputs
```

不要从“价值链”直接跳到“方法论工具”。中间必须先完成两次翻译：  
Do not jump directly from value chain to methodology tools. First complete two translations:

```text
价值链 -> 关键组织能力 -> 组织形式与组织机制 -> 方法论工具
Value chain -> key organizational capabilities -> organization forms and mechanisms -> methodology tools
```

组织形式包括：事业部制、职能制、矩阵制、项目制、区域制、平台/中台、共享服务、敏捷小队等。  
Organization forms include business units, functional organization, matrix, project-based organization, regional model, platform/middle office, shared services, and agile squads.

组织机制包括：权责分配、决策机制、协同流程、经营例会、预算机制、资源配置、绩效问责、项目治理、人才流动机制等。  
Organization mechanisms include accountability, decision rights, collaboration process, business review cadence, budgeting, resource allocation, performance review, project governance, and talent mobility.

#### 第二层：方法论与工具 / Layer 2: Methodologies and Tools

用成熟工具把分析框架落地。优先使用头部咨询公司和成熟管理实践中的工具。  
Operationalize the framework with mature tools, especially top consulting firm and management-practice tools.

常用工具 / Common tools:

- 价值链分析 / Value chain analysis
- 商业模式与利润模型拆解 / Business model and profit model decomposition
- 组织能力地图 / Organizational capability map
- 目标运营模型 / Target Operating Model
- 组织健康诊断 / Organization health diagnosis
- RACI / RAPID / 决策权矩阵 / Decision-rights matrix
- 管理跨度与组织层级分析 / Span-and-layer analysis
- 角色与岗位价值评估 / Role and position valuation
- 关键岗位识别 / Key position identification
- 人才分层与九宫格 / Talent segmentation and 9-box
- 能力模型与成功画像 / Competency model and success profile
- 人力规划 / Workforce planning
- 绩效指标树 / BSC / OKR / KPI / Performance indicator tree
- 激励有效性分析 / Incentive effectiveness analysis
- 流程诊断与服务 SLA / Process diagnosis and service SLA
- 变革影响分析和利益相关方分析 / Change impact and stakeholder analysis

每个工具都要说明：定义、适用场景、输入数据、操作步骤、输出结果、HRD 判断标准、支持的决策、误用风险、管理层可能质疑。  
For every tool, explain definition, use cases, input data, steps, outputs, HRD judgment criteria, decisions supported, misuse risks, and possible management challenges.

#### 第三层：实践与问题 / Layer 3: Practice and Problems

把框架和工具转成真实落地问题。  
Translate frameworks and tools into real implementation issues.

必须包含 / Must include:

- 真实业务场景 / Real business context
- 相关角色和利益关系 / Stakeholders and interests
- 关键冲突点 / Conflict points
- 业务方阻力 / Business resistance
- 数据质量和口径争议 / Data quality and definition disputes
- 跨部门冲突 / Cross-functional conflict
- 管理者能力不足 / Manager capability gaps
- 会议和沟通机制 / Meeting and communication mechanisms
- 法律、合规和员工关系风险 / Legal, compliance, and employee relations risks
- 常见低水平做法和高质量做法 / Low-quality and high-quality practices
- 最终沉淀成什么制度、流程、模板、指标或人才机制 / What policy, process, template, metric, or talent mechanism should be institutionalized

### 4. 搜索与证据收集 / Search and Evidence Collection

涉及当前信息、外部环境、法律、市场、公司、行业趋势时，必须查找资料。  
Browse or research when the topic involves current information, external environment, law, market, companies, or industry trends.

#### 搜索增强协议 / Search Enhancement Protocol

目标不是“搜得多”，而是提升四种密度：

The goal is not to search more, but to increase four kinds of density:

1. 证据密度：每个核心结论至少有 2 个来源，或 1 个强权威来源支撑。  
   Evidence density: every core conclusion should have at least two sources, or one highly authoritative source.
2. 方法论密度：每个重要框架都要找到可操作的方法论工具，而不是只停留在概念。  
   Methodology density: every important framework should connect to actionable methodology tools, not just concepts.
3. 实践问题密度：必须搜索真实落地中的阻力、失败案例、争议和限制。  
   Practice-problem density: search for real implementation resistance, failures, disputes, and limitations.
4. 反证密度：必须主动寻找反例、批评观点或不适用场景，防止结论过度乐观。  
   Counter-evidence density: actively search for counterexamples, criticism, or contexts where the idea does not apply.

搜索资料必须整理成四类：

Search findings must be organized into four categories:

| 类别 / Category | 作用 / Purpose | 示例 / Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 事实证据 / Factual Evidence | 支撑问题判断和结论 / Support diagnosis and conclusions | 行业数据、法规、公司公告、研究报告 / Industry data, regulation, company filings, research reports |
| 方法论工具 / Methodology Tools | 让分析框架可落地 / Operationalize the framework | TOM、RACI/RAPID、能力模型、九宫格 / TOM, RACI/RAPID, competency model, 9-box |
| 实践问题 / Practice Problems | 揭示落地阻力 / Reveal implementation barriers | 业务反对、数据争议、部门墙、干部不执行 / Business resistance, data disputes, silos, weak execution |
| 反证材料 / Counter-Evidence | 校正结论边界 / Clarify limits and risks | 失败案例、争议观点、不适用条件 / Failure cases, criticism, non-applicable conditions |

搜索之后必须回到主框架中消化：

After searching, map findings back into the core framework:

```text
搜索材料 / Search findings
-> 价值链 / Value Chain
-> 关键组织能力 / Key Organizational Capabilities
-> 组织形式与组织机制 / Organization Forms and Mechanisms
-> 关键岗位与核心人才 / Key Positions and Core Talent
-> HR 机制输出 / HR Mechanism Outputs
-> 实践问题与风险控制 / Practice Problems and Risk Control
```

禁止把搜索材料直接堆成正文。必须提炼为判断、机制、工具、数据口径、实践问题和风险边界。  
Do not paste search material directly into the final output. Convert it into judgments, mechanisms, tools, data definitions, practical problems, and risk boundaries.

优先级 / Source priority:

- 法律法规、政府机构、官方统计 / Laws, government agencies, official statistics
- 公司公告、官网、投资者材料、年报 / Company announcements, official websites, investor materials, annual reports
- 头部咨询公司、专业协会、研究机构 / Top consulting firms, professional bodies, research institutes
- 学术研究或同行评审资料 / Academic or peer-reviewed research
- 可信商业媒体 / Reputable business media
- 供应商文章只用于工具生态或产品特性，并标注“供应商视角” / Vendor content only for tool ecosystems or product features; label as vendor perspective

### 5. 综合判断 / Synthesis

把证据转成 HRD 能用的洞察。  
Convert evidence into HRD-usable insight.

回答 / Answer:

- 这个问题本质上解决什么经营问题？ / What business problem does it solve?
- CEO 会关心什么？ / What would the CEO care about?
- 业务负责人可能抵触什么？ / What might business leaders resist?
- 需要什么 HR 数据？ / What HR data is needed?
- 能支持什么管理决策？ / What management decision does it support?
- 哪个机制能让它真正落地？ / What mechanism makes it stick?
- 如果做错，会出现什么风险？ / What risks appear if done poorly?

### 6. 质量检查 / Quality Bar

最终输出前检查 / Before finalizing, check:

- 是否回答了用户真实目标？ / Does it answer the user's real objective?
- 是否区分事实、来源综合、专业推断和上下文假设？ / Are facts, source-based synthesis, professional inference, and assumptions separated?
- 是否有清晰的问题树或逻辑图？ / Is there a clear issue tree or logic map?
- 是否清楚呈现三层结构？ / Is the three-layer structure clear?
- 是否完整呈现价值链 -> 组织能力 -> 组织形式/机制 -> 关键岗位/人才 -> HR 机制输出？ / Does it cover value chain -> capabilities -> organization forms/mechanisms -> key roles/talent -> HR mechanism outputs?
- 是否执行搜索增强协议，并把搜索结果整理为事实证据、方法论工具、实践问题和反证材料？ / Has the search enhancement protocol been applied, with findings organized as factual evidence, methodology tools, practice problems, and counter-evidence?
- 每个重要观点是否回答经营问题、适用阶段、验证数据、责任人、动作顺序、阻力、成功判断和副作用？ / Does every important point cover business problem, applicable stage, validation data, owner, action sequence, resistance, success criteria, and side effects?
- 方法论工具是否展开到定义、适用场景、操作步骤、输入数据、输出结果、HRD 判断标准、误用风险和管理层质疑？ / Are tools expanded into definition, scenarios, steps, inputs, outputs, HRD judgment, misuse risks, and management challenges?
- 是否把口号换成了具体操作细节？ / Are slogans converted into operational details?
